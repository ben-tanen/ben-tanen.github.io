In the lead up to Election Night 2020, I made [”Where Is Election Night 2020 Heading?”]({% post_url 2020-11-03-election-night-funnel %}), a live updating graphic to keep track how much reality aligned with FiveThirtyEight’s forecast. Amongst all of the chaos, this was my data-viz-nerd way of making sense of it all…

This time around I had to do something similar, so I decided to make a 2024 remake of [this graphic from 2020](https://observablehq.com/@observablehq/electoral-college-decision-tree), which itself is a remake of [this graphic from 2012](https://archive.nytimes.com/www.nytimes.com/interactive/2012/11/02/us/politics/paths-to-the-white-house.html).

So I present [”2024 Electoral College Decision Tree”](https://observablehq.com/d/16c348efd72f110c), or “Where Is Election Night 2024 Heading.” Let’s see how it goes!!
