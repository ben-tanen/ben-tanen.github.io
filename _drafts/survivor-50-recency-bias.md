The [cast of Survivor’s upcoming 50th season was announced today](https://ew.com/survivor-50-cast-contestants-revealed-11742493), and since S50 is the first season of Survivor’s “new era” (the years after Survivor’s all-winners S40, which happened to align with COVID shutdowns in 2020) with all returning players. Since the announcement, there has been discussion about how the cast of S50 has some [recency bias](https://www.reddit.com/r/survivor/comments/1kx9zft/every_returnee_cast_has_had_recency_bias/) towards the most recent seasons–in particular S48, which just finished and has 3 players representing. I decided to dig into the data a bit more, just to explore how prevalent this recency bias is.
