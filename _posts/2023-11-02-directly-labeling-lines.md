Back in 2021, I came across [Harry Steven’s Observable notebook](https://observablehq.com/@harrystevens/directly-labelling-lines) outlining an automated approach for intelligently directly labeling line series. As with anyone, I’ve made my fair share of spaghetti plots where labeling data can be somewhat complicated, so I really liked Harry’s fairly clear, straightforward, yet quite smart approach. Thus, I had to learn how to implement it and why not do it via a tutorial that I can reference later - [it worked last time](https://observablehq.com/@ben-tanen/a-tutorial-to-using-d3-force-from-someone-who-just-learned-ho)!

Thus, I present [my recreation of Harry Steven’s tutorial for directly labeling lines](https://observablehq.com/d/4ea5db8903f39b8d)!
