In response to a suggestion from [Franklin Leonard](https://twitter.com/franklinleonard), the Washington Post recently published [a video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uHrvODeA8-E) providing an hour-by-hour comparison of how President Obama and President Trump responded in the wake of school shootings. The video is a very compelling piece for comparing (and contrasting) the differences in these two presidents' approaches.

The video did a great job juxtaposing Obama's and Trump's intraday activities and responses (comparing Obama's first day of response to Trump's, etc.), but I felt it was hard to compare the actual timing of each event. I decided to re-visualize <span id="footnote-1" class="footnote">this information</span> into one single timeline to (hopefully) present a slightly different effect.
